Who we are

The Faculty of Science and Technology Student Society (FSTSS) is the official student society representing students within the Faculty of Science and Technology at The University of the West Indies, St. Augustine Campus.

We are committed to creating a vibrant, inclusive, and supportive community where students can connect, grow, and make the most of their university experience. Through academic initiatives, social events, professional development opportunities, and student-focused activities, we aim to bridge the gap between students, the Faculty, and the wider university community.

Our society provides students with opportunities to develop their skills, build meaningful connections, gain practical experience, and engage with their peers beyond the classroom. We also advocate for the interests and well-being of students while creating opportunities for them to contribute to the FST community.

Whether you're looking to get involved, develop your leadership skills, meet new people, or simply make your university experience more memorable, FSTSS is a community built for you.
